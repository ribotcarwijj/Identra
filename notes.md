## Identra 0.7.35

- **Update check fixed**: Identra now checks for updates every time you open it and shows this changelog with an "Update now" button. Also in Settings > About: "Check for updates" and an "Auto-check updates" switch.
- **New app picker for instances** (0.7.34): search, app icons, selected apps on top, "Show system apps" switch.
- **Keyboard**: your keyboard (e.g. Gboard) is ticked by default for a new instance and can be unticked.
